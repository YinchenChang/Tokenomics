## Nebius（NBIS）

### MW 定義（一手全文）
- **Form 20-F FY2025（2026-04-30，`nbis-20251231x20f.htm`，業務章節〈Data center footprint〉）。** 「We primarily define our data center capacity in three ways」：
  - 「Contracted power is capacity that has been secured by land and contracted power commitments.」
  - 「Connected power is capacity that has power connected into data centers」
  - 「Active power is capacity being consumed by IT equipment and available for revenue generation. As of December 31, 2025, we had approximately 170 MW of active power capacity across the globe」
- **Q1 2026 股東信（6-K 2026-05-13 附件 99.2，`tm2614392d1_ex99-2.htm`）。** 定義改寫為：(1) contracted＝「power secured by contracted land and power commitments」；(2) connected＝「power connected into fully built and equipped data centers」；(3) active＝「power being consumed by installed, operational IT equipment and available for revenue generation」。
- **Q4 2025 股東信（6-K 2026-02-12 附件 99.2，`tm266173d1_ex99-2.htm`）。** 較短版本：active＝「power being consumed by IT equipment」；「We ended the year with ~170 MW of active power, significantly exceeding our 100 MW target」。
- **Q3 2025 股東信（6-K 2025-11-11 附件 99.2，`tm2530882d1_ex99-2.htm`）。** 「We are on track for 220 MW of connected power and 100 MW of active power by the end of 2025」；圖註 connected power「Includes power that is fully provisioned and can be activated immediately upon GPU installation」。
- **Q2 2025 股東信（6-K 附件 99.2，`tm2522866d1_ex99-2.htm`）。** connected power「includes both active power and power that is fully provisioned in our data centers and can be activated immediately upon GPU installation」。
- 四份文件都沒有寫 IT 關鍵電力或設施電力、沒有 PUE。

### 營收與 ARR（一手全文）
- Q4 2025 股東信：「Core AI cloud revenue was $214.2 million in Q4'25」；ARR「$1.25 billion as of the end of December 2025 … more than doubled from $551 million reported as of the end of September」；註 (1) ARR＝最後一個月營收 × 12。
- 季末 active MW：Q1、Q2 2026 股東信、新聞稿、6-K 財報皆未揭露（Q2 信只說「brought additional capacity online … during the second half of the second quarter」）。Q2 2026 ARR 30 億美元（6 月 × 12）無對應 MW。

### 合約單價（一手全文，Q2 2026 股東信，6-K 2026-08-12 附件 99.2，`tm2622968d1_ex99-2.htm`）
- 第 2 頁圖：「ACV per MW is stepping up(1) Q2 deals >$20M Q3 short-term capacity deals >$40M 2026 base $12M (2)(3)」；「Payback period 1 year 10 months」。
- 註：「(1) ACV — Annual contract value. (2) Methodology is on a revenue recognition basis, excluding any prepayment. (3) Estimated payback period for new deals signed in Q2, based on forecast costs and contracted future capacity, including capacity not yet built.」
- 第 3 頁：「We closed four landmark deals … for an average total contract value (TCV) of more than $1 billion each, and a yield of $20-25 million per megawatt」；「We see a price opportunity in the $40-50 million per MW range and we signed our first one this week」；「the expected payback period for the associated capex and related operating costs for Q2 deals is 1 year and 10 months, down from our two-to-three year payback period previously」。
- 首次容量拍賣：「securing the highest price we have cleared for NVIDIA Blackwell chips to date」。Nasdaq／blockspace 法說摘要（摘述）：拍賣價高於先前 Blackwell 最高價 15%、高於管線價 20%；四筆大單期限 1–3 年；短期約至多 6 個月。
- 機隊世代（Q4 2025 信）：「We started deploying NVIDIA B300 and NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 systems in 2025」；2025-11 歐洲首家量產 HGX B300。
