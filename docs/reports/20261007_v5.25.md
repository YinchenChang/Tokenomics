## 六、Epoch `Confidence` 定義查核
**查得**。出處：Epoch AI 資料說明頁 `https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models-documentation/records`，Confidence 欄：「Metadata describing our confidence in the recorded values for Training compute, Parameters, and Training dataset size.」Likely 的定義：「Similar to confident in terms of methodology, but the inputs to the methodology are not as reliable.」（Confident：「…based on primary or reliable sources who are providing specific technical details…」；Speculative：「Our best guess…may change substantially as more information comes to light.」）該頁**沒有**給各等級的數值倍數區間。已寫入 SRC_MOD_055 備註與 E248 備註；未據此推定區間。
