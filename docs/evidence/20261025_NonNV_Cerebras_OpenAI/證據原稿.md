## Cerebras

### C01 The Register（2026-08-19）「Cerebras CS-4 rack systems juice chips for every last drop of AI performance」
https://www.theregister.com/a/5289286
- "The WSE-3 was already a hot chip at 15 kW at the wafer level and around 23 kW at the system level."
- 附表 WSE-3：SRAM 44 GB；Sparse FP16 125 PFLOPS；Dense FP16 12.5 PFLOPS；記憶體頻寬 21.6 PB/s；TDP（wafer）15 kW；TDP（System）23 kW
- 附表 WSE-3T：Sparse 250／Dense 25 PFLOPS；43.2 PB/s；TDP（System）"46kW estimated"
- "Cerebras' headline performance figure relies heavily on sparsity, which as a general rule doesn't benefit LLM inference."

- CS-4：每台最多 3 個 "backpack"；機架功率 Cerebras 未說明，記者估 120–140 kW；"expects the first CS-4-based systems to come online later this quarter"
