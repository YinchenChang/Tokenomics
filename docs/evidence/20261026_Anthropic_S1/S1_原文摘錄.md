## Broadcom 設備租賃（Infrastructure Equipment Leases）

> Infrastructure Equipment Leases We have entered into five-year master equipment leases with Broadcom, pursuant to which we receive such hardware for deployment in dedicated data center facilities operated by others on our behalf (the “Lease Arrangements”). These Lease Arrangements are accounted for as finance leases. Our rent payments over the life of these leases are equal to an aggregate of approximately $161.2 billion excluding interest, with rent payable in quarterly installments and payment obligations typically commencing upon AI infrastructure equipment becoming “ready for service” at the applicable data center facility. Rent payments may commence earlier in certain circumstances, such as when the readiness of a data center facility is delayed. The Lease Arrangements provide us with access to TPU-based compute capacity, a portion of which began to come online in 2026, with additional capacity expected to come online starting in 2027.
